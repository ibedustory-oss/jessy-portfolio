02/Try it yourself

A Why comes out of these four.

A Why is not a flash of insight. It is what these four checks produce. The row you cannot fill in is the weakest part of the business right now.

Audience

Who, and in what state afterwards

Common answer

People who want to get healthier

Usable answer

A year after giving birth, someone whose back hurts too much to lift their child can lift them again

Pain and gain

Why you, rather than anyone else

Common answer

Because we love pilates

Usable answer

I spent a year with the same pain after my own birth. I know in my body which movements make it worse

Market

How many of them are there, and is that growing

Common answer

The pilates market is growing

Usable answer

This district records N births a year, and M of those families live within walking distance

Competition

Who is already standing in that spot

Common answer

It is crowded, but we win on quality

Usable answer

All three studios nearby run group classes. Nobody does one-to-one postnatal work

Once the four are filled in, you know what the site should show first.

03/What I built

Concept demos

Korean restaurant / Paris 1er

JOAYO

Audience
Someone working in Paris can come in alone on a weeknight, eat rice and soup, and leave.
Pain and gain
We can serve the meal we ate at home every day. We know the ordinary side of this food, not the occasion side.
Market
Korean places in the 1st arrondissement run on tourist barbecue, and the weeknight table for one is not counted.
Competition
Everything nearby is grilled meat and hotpot. Nobody builds around rice and soup.

Pilates / Paris

MILATES

Audience
Someone whose exercise never lasted can put into words what is happening in their own body.
Pain and gain
The teacher comes out of physiotherapy and can explain, every time, why this movement and not another.
Market
Pilates in Paris is compared on machine count and price, and there is nowhere for people who want the reasoning.
Competition
The studios nearby run group classes, and the teacher's thinking never reaches the surface.
